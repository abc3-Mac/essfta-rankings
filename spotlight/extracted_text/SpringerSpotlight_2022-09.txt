ENGLISH SPRINGER SPANIEL FOUNDATION
Randy Capsel
Communications and Outreach Report:
Feedback received that was related to Foundation sponsored events at the National Specialty
indicated a great deal of positive feedback and the Foundation sincerely hopes that the Breeder
Showcase, eye exam availability, and the CGC and Trick Dog test event added enjoyable time
and useful information for those able to participate.
Dr. Miyadera, while conducting eye examinations during the week, saw over 100 new dogs and
between 30 and 40 repeat examinations. These examinations will provide increased potential to
the ongoing study. Related to the ongoing study, the Foundation recently began reviewing
information and proposals for possible extension of the current study by Dr. Miyadera. Much
valuable information is being obtained at this stage of the project. Various considerations are
being discussed and over the next few months the Foundation will be determining how to
possibly support continuation of the project. To date the Foundation has provided over $99,303
of financial support for the ongoing study.
The Foundation has also provided over $30,000 to the AKC Canine Health Fund (CHF) tick-
borne disease research initiative. This ongoing research is an important One Health related
initiative that will not only provide improved knowledge for the veterinary medicine and diagnostic
field, but also increases knowledge available for identifying and treating the associated diseases
in humans.
The Foundation has also initiated discussions with researchers at the University of Minnesota
who are involved in study involving immune-mediated hemolytic anemia. The current study is
currently ongoing under an AKC CHF grant that has a grant period listed from January 1, 2022,
through December 31, 2023. The Foundation is conducting internal discussions to assess
financial assistance for this important research which may assist in identifying a major
contributing factor to this disorder. More information will follow in the future regarding this project
and Foundation financial support.

ENGLISH SPRINGER SPANIEL FOUNDATION
Randy Capsel
More in-depth information will be forthcoming in the Foundation Annual Report, but a review of
the Foundation’s research support history to the AKC CHF indicates the Foundation has provided
$245,445.00 of financial support of various canine health projects. This support encompasses a
time period of August 1999 to the current time. The supported studies have related to canine
epilepsy, progressive retinal atrophy, non-Hodgkin lymphoma, mean seeds/grass awns, bloat,
and tick-borne diseases. The Foundation wishes to thank the past support from so many English
Springer Spaniel owners, breeders, and admirers whom have allowed the Foundation to provide
such important funding.
If there are any specific objectives or information you want me to focus upon, please let me know
and I will do my best to update accordingly.

Thoughts from the Field
Mike Nolan, ESSFT VP of the Field
Introduction:
The purpose of this short memo is to increase communication among the Clubs. There is no agenda
here. It is to keep issues in front of us to help facilitate communication and direction to help improve
our game. I plan to do this from time to time with really no programmed timing. Let me know how you
feel about this. Please feel free to comment on anything that you feel the Board of Governors needs to
adjust need to address.
Changing of Apprentice Program:
Changes to the Apprentice program were approved and were implemented effective 26 Jul 22. They will
be posted to EESFTA.Org and will be posted to the web sites such as ESSFT.com shortly. I will summa-
rize them quickly here for your information:
1. Committee will design a random question Test.
A.. The Apprentice Program Coordinator will form a Committee
B. The Committee will work on questions and design more than one test.
2. The Apprentice Program Coordinator (APC) will manage the process.
A. Verify applicant
B. Coach the applicant on best practice/next steps and monitor them throughout the pro-
cess.
C. Recruit, Coordinate and Schedule the Judging Apprenticeships.
D. Move the Applicant to the Approved Judges List when complete.
E. Help Coordinate the 1st Judging Assignment with the Sponsor Judge.
f. Other items as needed for the program.
3. The applicant must pass the Judges Test.
A. The test shall be requested from the APC and must be returned in 30 days.
B. A test retake can be every four (4) months
C. After three (3) test failures, the applicant must attend an additional Judges Seminar.
4. The following requirement must be met by US Citizens before becoming an US Springer Judge:
A. Complete five AKC Cocker Spaniel Field Trial or AKC Springer Puppy Field Trial Judging
Assignments or any combination of both.
B. Complete 3 Apprentice assignments for AKC ESS Field Trials
C. Qualify for 3 National Championships (NAC, NOC or combination).
D. Compete in AKC ESS Field Trials for a minimum of three (3) years.

6. When the program standard is met, the APC will move the applicant Judge to the approved

A. If a prospective Judge has completed the seminar and test but has NOT apprenticed
judged they would be required to do 3 apprenticeships, one of which will be outside of
their interclub.
B. The Prospective Apprentice Judge that has started the current program and has complet-
ed one apprenticeship shall complete the existing program within twenty-four (24)
months. (Note: As approved on July 26, 2022, the current Apprentice Judges qualifying
for this requirement shall have until July 26, 2024 to complete the program and become
an approve English Springer Spaniel Field Trial Judge)
Is this perfect….no. What we are trying to do is trying to continue to improve what we do. I am
sure there are items which we can do better and there will be unintended consequences for
some of these changes we need to be aware of them quickly and makes changes where neces-
sary.

Thoughts from the Field (Cont.)
Mike Nolan, ESSFT VP of the Field
NAC 2022: I just came back from Judging in Utah a nd walked the National course for the NAC this
year. I was concerned because of the drought. It looks far better than I thought it would. I am encour-
aged and God willing they will have a little rain and h elp us out. Bob Childs and his team are on top of
it and they are very organized. I would like to encourage you all to think about running it. I think it will
be a great time.
FT Gunning: We have had great support for gunning seminars across the country and I have attended
the NJ seminar and will attend the Mid-East in Michigan this weekend. Morgan and the Interclub Chairs
have done a great job here. There is considerable disinformation about gunning. Most of our guns are
very good. We need to encourage them to attend…shoot training and improve. I have seen marked
improvement in the gunning and it will continue with Trial committees insisting on it. We are working
on getting the Gunners list.
Trial management: is a concern for some of us. We are having turnover in Committee leadership
and this is having a negative effect. The power in our organization is the Field Trial Committee. We
need to make sure we teach the new leadership that details matter. Trial layout…..Bird Planting, park-
ing and water availability all have an impact on our game. Please think for this as you plan your trial.
Start thinking about who on your committee can be the next Field Trial Chair. Get him/her involved.
We will have a ‘Best Practice’ section on the web site with helpful information.
Mentor Program: We had a short discussion concerning the “Judge Mentoring Program” that Bob
Iverson is interested in. I think we owe it to Bob to have him present it to the Board himself at our
next Zoom meeting. We may want to make it “Optional” and see how it works. Let’s get Bob to dis-
cuss. Let me know your feelings on this. Bob would be willing to do it.
Just a final thought. This is a great game. Can it be frustrating….yes but I had the privilege of watch-
ing great dogs run in the most beautiful location with some of the nicest folks in the US. I saw this dog
“Bandit” find a hen in thick cover on a creek bottom and run it down through really thick undercover
and drive it out and sit perfectly as the bird burst out into the open. The gun shot it and Bandit sat and
waited for his handler to make it to him with the judge and drove out and returned it to hand. There
may be other reasons to do this. There may be frustrations and political BS but those 3 minutes makes
it worth it.
IT’S ALL ABOUT THE DOGS!
