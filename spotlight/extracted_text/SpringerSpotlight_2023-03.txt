2023 National Pheasant Fest & Quail Classic
By Cristy Joy, ESSFTA President
ESSFTA Members share their Love of the English Springer Spaniel at the 2023 National Pheas-
ant Fest & Quail Classic in Minneapolis Minnesota
Pheasants Forever and Quail Forever’s 2023, National Pheasant Fest & Quail Classic, in
Minneapolis MN had an increase of 46 percent over the 2020 show which makes this the largest
show floor in Pheasant Fest and Quail Classic history, and every bit of it was filled with exhibitor
booth space, stages, activities, people and of course dogs.
“Saturday is always an exciting day a Pheasant Fest, and this year did not disappoint,” said Jar-
ed Wiklund, Pheasants Forever and Quail Forever’s public relations manager. “The show floor
was packed all day and our seminar stages were full of great classes, tutorials and panel discus-
sions.”
The 2023 Pheasant and Quail Classic was a great success for our English Springer Spaniel
Field Trial Association organization with our members, Shane and Wendy Kozal participating in
the ESSFTA booth. They shared the love of the English Springer Spaniel breed. The passion of
our group with our flushing sporting dogs attracted outdoor enthusiasts and hunters. We also
increased the awareness of “Mean Seeds” to help protect the canine community. We had great
participation from our Membership. Our ESSFTA professional photographers donated pictures
for the display booth. We had pictures from Mark with Geeb Photography, Katie Gorecki
Photography and the banner picture is by Kim Kulman. Additional ESSFTA members from the
area stopped by to say ‘Hi’ and share their Springer stories. It was great to have our Members at
the Event!!
The National Pheasant Fest & Quail Classic combines a national consumer show, wildlife habitat
seminar series, and family event complete with puppies, tractors, shotguns and wildlife art.
Pheasant Fest is the country’s largest event for upland hunters, sport dog owners and wildlife
habitat conservationists.
Next year, National Pheasant Fest & Quail Classic will be in Sioux Falls, South Dakota. Mark
your calendars for an excited opportunity to dive into the world of Upland hunting.
– Cristy K. Joy, ESSFTA Board Member At-Large and Corresponding Secretary
*Source for information was from the News Release by Pheasant Forever Media Contact Chad Love

Thoughts From The Field
Submitted by Mike Nolan, Field Vice President
B ird Dog Hall of Fame: The Bird Dog Hall of Fame located in Grand Junction TN is a fabulous place.
Each February an induction is held to honor exceptional Bird Dogs of all breeds and the p eople who im-
pact the sport we love. This year Mike Elsasser from Ohio was inducted along with Fred Munsone’s
fabulous dog NAFC FC AFC CFC Flushing Wings Annie and Al Beede’s dog NAFC Cathy’s Kris of Burnsget.
The question often comes up “How Do I nominate a person of Dog for the Hall of Fame?” . I will try to
outline the process for all.
The dog nominated needs to be deceased. There are NO other hard and fast criteria. What the com-
mittee considers can be summarized as follows. There are Good Dogs….there are Great d ogs…we are
looking for the exceptional dog! Longevity with success are key ingredients for consideration. The com-
mittee takes this responsibility with a great deal of serious discussion. Back up data and supporting docu-
mentation….National Placements…High Point awards….Total points all help when the com mittee is in
discussion.
An individual to be nominated MUST be at least 65 years of age and have contributed to the enhance-
ment of the game. Letters of recommendation and an outline of accomplishments (Committee assign-
ments, Chairmanships. Gunning responsibilities etc. are all considered by the committee).
To initiate a recommendation for either a dog or a person a simple email to the committee chairman
(mnolan4862@comcast.net) prior to October 15th will get the list started for consideration. Backup
material is needed by Thanksgiving so that there is time for committee discussion.
A person or dog will remain on the list if they receive at least one vote from a committee member and
will automatically be considered again the following year.
As a committee we want to make sure that we do not miss the Dogs and the Men and Women who
should be recognized. Feel free to nominate those leaders in your interclub that deserve this great
honor.
Parent Club Trial 2024: Just a reminder that the 100th anniversary of the Parent Club trial will be held in
NY in October of 2024. We would like to have this well supported. The trophies awarded at this trial are
remarkable and go back to the Kennel Club of the UK. We are looking for National Quality Judges and
want to honor the history of our game in North America. Stay tuned for further announcements.
Judging Nationals: The Board of Governors will be discussing enhanced criteria for qualifications for
judging National Championship Trials. This will be on our agenda at our meeting in Minneapolis in May. If
anyone has any thoughts on this subject, please let your Governors know prior to the first weekend in
May.

Therapy Dogs and English Springer Spaniels
by Judy Manley
The versatility of our breed is one of the aspects that makes us love them so much. They hunt in the fields which
shows their purpose, show in conformation which shows their beauty, do performance which shows their intelli-
gence and agile capabilities, and most recently, they have been proven to excel in scent detection.
One of the least common gifts these dogs give to us is their ability to console and do therapy work. Many people
have done hospital visits and nursing home visits but one beautiful dog I know of holds the distinguished therapy
title with the AKC. Ch. Shala’s Can’t Take My Eyes Off Of You a.k.a. Blake, owned by Raymond Ritchotte. Ray and
Blake belong to three organizations doing pet therapy. Blake has earned the title from the AKC of “Distinguished
Therapy Dog”. They have made over
585 visits to comfort children and families in need or hospitalized. Fifteen more visits and Blake will be the first Eng-
lish Springer Spaniel with the AKC title “Therapy Dog Supreme.”
One group of special note is Gabriel’s Angels a 501c3 organization. Their pet therapy program is the only Arizona
program that offers healing pet therapy to at-risk children. Blake and Ray specialize in Autistic children. Gabriel’s
Angels says the difference they have made to the children is nothing short of phenomenal.
Blake and Ray have been so successful because this breed is exceptional. We have a special breed here and if you
see Blake and Ray, give them a thumbs up and thank Ray for sharing his wonderful springer with the children and
making this world a kinder place.

Bronze: Westminster winner: CH Felicity’s Diamond Jim “James”
Artist, Ruth Dehmel
My first experience shopping the wide array of springer arts, crafts, and treasures was at a National in Allentown, PA
several decades ago. There, I met artist, Ruth Dehmel, and acquired two gorgeous pieces of her jewelry designs, a
group of her exquisite ceramics, and several of her trademark sweet and conformation-correct tee shirts.
Dehmel (Rendition) finds inspiration capturing the spirit and conformation of her lovely dogs. She has “always loved
dogs and the ESS has owned my heart and been constantly inspiring to my artist’s eye since exhibiting my first show
Springer in 1973. My favorite mediums are clay and sculpting. My favorite piece of work is my bronze sculpture of Ch.
Felicity Diamond Jim. I work in my small studio; busy creating new sculptures and jewelry. I invite jewelry and sculp-
ture lovers to visit my booth at the 2023 National at Purina Farm (facebook.com/groups/296406517230796). The best
way to contact me about my art is by private Facebook message.
